Chrona

PRIVACY POLICY

Overview

Chrona is a world clock app that displays the current time in cities around the world. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Short version: Chrona does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

Chrona does not collect or transmit:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any data to external servers

The app has no internet permission and makes no network requests.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Chrona stores the following preferences only on your device using Android's DataStore:

  • Your selected wheel cities and their order
  • Your home timezone setting
  • Your preferred time format (12h / 24h)
  • Your preferred theme (Light / Dark / System)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android Settings.

Permissions

Chrona does not request any sensitive Android permissions. It does not access:

  • Camera, microphone, or contacts
  • Location (GPS or network-based)
  • Storage / files
  • Internet or network state

Children's Privacy

Chrona is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else, as the app collects no data at all.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the new version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
