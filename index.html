PRIVACY POLICY

Overview

Chrona is a world clock app that displays the current time in cities around the world. This privacy policy explains what data is collected, by whom, and how it is used — both by Chrona itself and by the third-party services it integrates with.

1. Data Collected by Chrona

Chrona itself does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The app does not have its own backend or analytics pipeline. Your preferences and settings stay on your device.

However, Chrona integrates third-party SDKs (advertising and crash reporting) that may collect certain data as described in sections 3 and 4 below.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Chrona stores the following preferences only on your device using Android's DataStore. This data is never sent to any server.

  • Your selected wheel cities and their display order
  • Your home timezone setting
  • Your preferred time format (12 h / 24 h)
  • Your preferred theme (Auto / Light / Dark / Dynamic)
  • Your selected accent colour
  • Your crash-reporting preference (enabled / disabled)

You can clear all locally stored data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Chrona → Storage → Clear Data.

CONTAINS ADS

3. Advertising — AppLovin MAX

Chrona displays banner advertisements served by AppLovin MAX. AppLovin is a third-party advertising platform that may collect and use data to deliver relevant ads.

What AppLovin may collect

  • Device identifiers — Google Advertising ID (GAID) and App Set ID
  • Device make, model, hardware, and operating system version
  • Screen size, orientation, and display properties
  • IP address and approximate location (country / region)
  • Time zone and locale (preferred language)
  • Network connection type and speed
  • App name, version, and session information
  • Advertising and tracking preferences you have set on your device

How AppLovin uses this data

AppLovin uses this data to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and improve its advertising platform. AppLovin may use the Google Advertising ID to serve personalised (interest-based) ads. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (see Section 8).

AppLovin's policies

AppLovin's data practices are governed by their own privacy policy: applovin.com/privacy. We encourage you to review it.

4. Crash Reports & Analytics

Chrona includes an optional crash-reporting feature to help us identify and fix bugs. This feature is opt-in and can be toggled in Settings → Privacy → Crash Reports & Analytics.

When enabled, anonymous crash and diagnostic data may be collected, including:

  • Stack traces and error messages at the time of a crash
  • Device model and OS version
  • App version

No personally identifiable information is included in crash reports. When the toggle is off (the default), no crash data is collected or transmitted.

5. Android Permissions

Chrona requests the following Android permissions:

Used

INTERNET AD_ID
  • INTERNET — Required to load and display banner advertisements from AppLovin MAX and, if enabled, to transmit crash reports.
  • AD_ID — Required by the AppLovin SDK to access the Google Advertising ID for ad personalisation (subject to your device ad preferences).

Not requested

LOCATION CAMERA MICROPHONE CONTACTS STORAGE SMS / CALL LOG

Chrona does not request any sensitive permissions beyond those listed above.

7. Data Sharing & Sale

Chrona (the developer) does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for their own marketing purposes.

Data collected by AppLovin MAX is shared with AppLovin Corporation and its advertising partners as described in AppLovin's privacy policy. Chrona has no control over that data once it is collected by the AppLovin SDK.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Opt out of personalised ads

You can limit ad tracking or reset your Advertising ID at any time:

  • Android 12+: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID
  • Older Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation

Disable crash reporting

Open Chrona → Settings → Privacy and disable the Crash Reports & Analytics toggle (off by default).

Delete locally stored data

Go to Android Settings → Apps → Chrona → Storage → Clear Data. This resets all your in-app preferences.

GDPR / CCPA

If you are located in the European Union, European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, or California, you may have additional rights regarding your personal data held by AppLovin. Please refer to AppLovin's Privacy Policy and use their opt-out mechanism to exercise those rights.

9. Children's Privacy

Chrona is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The AppLovin SDK is not configured in child-directed mode. If you believe a child has inadvertently provided personal data, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, the revised version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date. For material changes, we may notify users via an in-app notice.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
